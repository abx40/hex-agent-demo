Connect your edges

Take turns placing one stone on an empty hex. Red links the top and bottom. Blue links the left and right. The first chain of same-colour stones that share edges and connect both of that colour’s sides wins.

Swap rule

Red always opens. On turn two, Blue can play an empty hex or select the highlighted red stone to swap colours. The opening stone stays in place, and the original opener moves next as Blue.
